## Why the results are low

- **Crops can miss the sign.** A seed-17 sample of 12 Mohenjo-daro validation seals showed the cutter clipping sign strokes in M-24 and M-187. The forced-cut archive knows how many signs a seal contains, then pairs the resulting strips with the reference signs. An incorrectly placed strip can still get a label. We have no estimate of the rate without reviewed boxes.
- **Rare classes are harder.** CNN top-1 was 2/47 (4.3%) for labels with no real training crops, 12/89 (13.5%) at 1–4, 29/169 (17.2%) at 5–19, and 170/381 (44.6%) at 20 or more. Wells 740 and 2 account for 278 predictions across the 686 test positions, with 121 occurrences in the references. Real-crop training samples signs in proportion to their observed frequency.
- **Render transfer needs real examples.** The render-only checkpoint scored 13/350 (3.7%) on real validation crops. After fine-tuning, the raw CNN scored 110/350 (31.4%) on the same 350 crops. This comparison includes the combined effects of visual domain and crop quality.
- **Per-sign and per-seal objectives differ.** Weight 0.75 was selected for validation sign accuracy. Test top-1 increased by 18 correct sign positions, while exact matches went from 3/141 to 2/141. In a validation-only check, both marginal decoding and joint Viterbi decoding at 0.75 matched 2/67 complete sequences. Swapping decoders alone did not solve the issue on this validation set.

The next controlled test is a paired comparison of forced and reviewed hand-box crops on the same validation signs with the same CNN (#31). After that, compare a more balanced real-crop sampler with the present sampler on validation, reporting accuracy by sign frequency and whole-seal exact matches.
