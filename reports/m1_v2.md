## Why the results are low

- **Crops can miss the sign.** A seed-17 sample of 12 Mohenjo-daro validation seals showed the cutter clipping sign strokes in M-24 and M-187. The forced-cut archive knows how many signs a seal contains, then pairs the resulting strips with the reference signs. An incorrectly placed strip can still get a label. We have no estimate of the rate without reviewed boxes.
- **Rare classes are harder.** CNN top-1 was 2/47 (4.3%) for labels with no real training crops, 12/89 (13.5%) at 1–4, 29/169 (17.2%) at 5–19, and 170/381 (44.6%) at 20 or more. Wells 740 and 2 account for 278 predictions across the 686 test positions, with 121 occurrences in the references. Real-crop training samples signs in proportion to their observed frequency.
- **Render transfer needs real examples.** The render-only checkpoint scored 13/350 (3.7%) on real validation crops. After fine-tuning, the raw CNN scored 110/350 (31.4%) on the same 350 crops. This comparison includes the combined effects of visual domain and crop quality.
- **Per-sign and per-seal objectives differ.** Weight 0.75 was selected for validation sign accuracy. Test top-1 increased by 18 correct sign positions, while exact matches went from 3/141 to 2/141. In a validation-only check, both marginal decoding and joint Viterbi decoding at 0.75 matched 2/67 complete sequences. Swapping decoders alone did not solve the issue on this validation set.

The next controlled test is a paired comparison of forced and reviewed hand-box crops on the same validation signs with the same CNN (#31). If the reviewed crops help, rebuild and retrain on reviewed training crops. After that, compare a more balanced real-crop sampler with the present sampler on validation, reporting accuracy by sign frequency and whole-seal exact matches. Keep the test set frozen during these changes.

### Legacy reproduction and interpretation

The original legacy checkpoint was unavailable, so this comparison uses a new seed-0 small CNN fit with the current legacy scripts and Mahadevan labels. We pretrained for eight epochs on 20,850 rendered sign crops and fine-tuned for 15 epochs with 2,232 real training crops. Validation on 173 crops selected epoch 15. The Colab T4 run took 149.5 seconds including setup (PyTorch 2.14.1+cu130). The training and validation crops went to the temporary runtime. Both test evaluations ran locally after the weights were downloaded.

The matched comparison evaluates two complete readers on the same catalogue positions. Changes include the sign inventory, training collection, and image crop pipeline. The larger v2 test table above separately reports performance on all 141 v2 test seals.

To reproduce the legacy fit after installing the project dependencies:

```bash
python scripts/make_real_crops.py
python scripts/make_carved_synthetic.py
python scripts/train_sign_cnn.py --arch small --synthetic single --pretrain-epochs 8 --finetune-epochs 15 --seed 0 --device cuda --no-test --out legacy_reproduced_v1.pt
```
